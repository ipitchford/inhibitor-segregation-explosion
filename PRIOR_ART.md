**Closest prior work.**
- *Yoo (2026), Zenodo preprint:* reports a counterexample to the unrestricted Conjecture 3.6 using zeroth-order enzyme production and compartment fusion. No first-disproof claim is made here; the restricted question (no inflow, no fusion, no exit) was not found answered.
- *Anderson, Howells and Rojas La Luz (2026):* Observation 3.7 and its discussion suggest that without inflow reactions no destabilising feedback loop occurs. The present result shows that the slowing of an inhibitory multi-reactant reaction can itself destabilise; the rate inequality of Observation 3.7 is not disputed.
- *Components (all cited):* Kaplan–Sudbury–Nilsen (1975) and Hermann–Pfaffelhuber (2020, Cor. 2.7) for survival under binomial disasters; Bansaye–Pardo Millán–Smadi (2013) and Gallo–Roldán-Correa (2026) for random multiplicative catastrophes; Leskelä–Robert–Simatos (2009/2010) for Yule processes with scheduled removals; Brockwell–Gani–Resnick (1982), Economou (2004), Kapodistria–Phung-Duc–Resing (2016), Ben-Ari–Roitershtein–Schinazi (2019) for immigration with catastrophes; Kendall (1948), Cogburn–Torrez (1981), Bacaër–Ed-Darraz (2014) for birth–death processes in varying environments; Siegmund (1976) and Asmussen–Sigman (1996) for order duality.
- *Companion Evidence Press release (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23505123):* classifies when division prevents explosion (Conjecture 3.14 of the same source). Complementary, not overlapping.

**Verdict.** The chemical embeddings, their sharp classification, the bulk-versus-well-mixed comparison and the enclosure are new within the bounded search. A bounded search does not establish priority.
